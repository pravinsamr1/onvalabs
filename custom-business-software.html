Frequently asked questions

Why not just buy an off-the-shelf platform?

Often you should. Custom becomes the better option when configuration and workarounds stop closing the gap, when licence costs keep rising for features you do not use, or when the process itself is part of your competitive advantage. We will tell you honestly which side of that line you are on.

What will it cost and how long will it take?

Both depend on scope, so we quote after discovery rather than before. Work is broken into stages with fixed deliverables, so you approve each phase, can pause between them, and see something working early rather than at the end.

Who owns the software?

You do. Source code, repositories and infrastructure are yours and documented at handover, built on open frameworks and standard databases rather than a proprietary platform only we can maintain. Changing providers later stays possible.
