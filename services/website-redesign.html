Common questions

Will a redesign cost us our search rankings?

It can, when URLs change and nothing points the old addresses at the new ones. That is why mapping happens first: every existing page is matched to its replacement, and 301 redirects preserve link equity when addresses change. Nobody controls Google, so we monitor closely after launch, but the common cause of a traffic drop is avoidable.

Do we need a full redesign or just maintenance?

Often maintenance is enough. If the structure is sound and the problem is speed, broken forms, outdated content or missing security updates, website maintenance services fix that without a rebuild. A redesign makes more sense when the site cannot be made fast, cannot work properly on a phone, or no longer reflects what you sell.

Can we keep our existing content and CMS?

Usually some of it. We audit the current pages, keep the ones earning traffic or answering real customer questions, rewrite the weak ones and retire anything redundant. Staying on your current CMS is possible when it still suits your team. Where it is the reason updates are painful, we explain the alternatives before deciding.
