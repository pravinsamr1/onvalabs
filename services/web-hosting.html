Common questions

How do I know if shared hosting is enough?

Shared hosting suits most marketing sites, blogs and smaller online stores with steady traffic. If you run heavy applications, sustained high traffic, custom server software or strict compliance requirements, a VPS gives you dedicated resources and control.

Can you move my existing website across?

Yes. We copy your files and databases, test the site on our infrastructure, then update DNS to cut over. Migrations are usually scheduled outside business hours to keep any interruption brief and predictable.

Do you guarantee my site will never go offline?

No honest host can promise that. What we do is host in quality Sydney facilities, monitor availability continuously, keep tested daily backups, and respond quickly when something breaks so downtime stays short.
