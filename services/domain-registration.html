Common questions

Do I need an ABN to register a .com.au domain?

You need an Australian presence, typically through an ABN, ACN or registered trademark, plus a close and substantial connection between your entity and the name you want. We check your eligibility against auDA policy before lodging the application.

What is a direct .au domain?

Direct .au registration lets you hold a shorter name such as yourbusiness.au alongside or instead of yourbusiness.com.au. Many organisations register both to protect their brand and redirect one to the other so customers reach the same site.

Can you move my existing domain to Onva Labs?

Yes. We request the domain transfer code from your current registrar, verify the registrant details, and coordinate the change so your website and email keep working throughout. Existing registration time is carried across, not lost.
