Android App Development

What we deliver

Android holds the larger share of handsets in most Australian workplaces, warehouses and customer bases, yet it is often the platform that gets built second and suffers for it. Screen sizes vary wildly, older operating system versions linger on devices still in daily use, and manufacturer skins break layouts that looked fine in the emulator. Add Google Play's review and data safety requirements, and a rushed Android build turns into a support problem instead of a sales channel.

We design and build Android applications properly from the first commit. That means native development in Kotlin with Jetpack Compose where performance and hardware access matter, or React Native or Flutter when you need one codebase across Android and iOS on a tighter budget.

Common questions

Should I build Android first, or both platforms at once?

If your audience skews towards value and mid-range handsets, trades and field teams, or a broad consumer market, Android first usually wins. If you need both platforms on one budget, cross-platform with React Native or Flutter is worth costing out properly.

How do you handle the huge range of Android devices?

We agree on a supported device and operating system matrix at the start, build layouts that adapt to different screen sizes, and test on real hardware. That keeps quality predictable without paying to support every handset ever sold.

Can you take over an existing Android app?

Yes. We start with a code and architecture review, document what is there, then give you a clear plan covering urgent fixes, Play Store compliance gaps and the sensible order for future work.
