What we do

AI optimisation services

Six strands of work that together decide whether an answer engine understands your business well enough to mention it when someone asks a question you can answer.

AIO, GEO and AEO, plainly explained

Artificial Intelligence Optimisation, Generative Engine Optimisation and Answer Engine Optimisation are largely different names for the same discipline. So is Search Everywhere Optimisation. We charge for the work, not the acronym.

Google AI Overviews and AI Mode

Google AI Overviews summarise answers above the normal results, and AI Mode goes further. We structure pages so your content is readable, attributable and worth drawing from.

ChatGPT search visibility work

ChatGPT browses the live web when it answers. That favours pages with clear headings, direct answers near the top and a site that responds quickly to an automated request.

Perplexity and cited sources

Perplexity shows its sources beside every answer, which makes citation visible and measurable. We work on the clarity and credibility that make a page worth citing.

Bing AI search and Copilot

Bing AI search feeds Microsoft Copilot, so Bing indexing basics still matter. We check your site is crawled properly there rather than assuming Google coverage is enough.

LLM optimisation and measurement

LLM optimisation services mean little without evidence. We track branded mentions, referral traffic from AI tools and the questions where you appear, then report in plain English.

Common questions

Are AIO, GEO and AEO three different services?

No. AI Optimisation, Generative Engine Optimisation and Answer Engine Optimisation are largely different names for the same discipline, and Search Everywhere Optimisation is another. Agencies pick whichever term they prefer. The underlying work is consistent: make your content clear, structured and credible enough that an answer engine can read it, understand it and quote it correctly.

Can you guarantee we appear in AI Overviews or ChatGPT?

No, and treat anyone who does with caution. Google, OpenAI, Perplexity and Microsoft decide what their systems say, and those systems change constantly without notice. What we influence is whether your content is structured, accurate and credible enough to be a sensible source. We report what we observe rather than promising an outcome we cannot control.
