Common questions

Shopify or WooCommerce?

Shopify suits sellers who want hosted infrastructure and a smaller maintenance load. WooCommerce suits stores needing unusual pricing rules, deep WordPress content or tighter control of data. We recommend one after looking at your catalogue, margins and internal capacity.

Can you migrate our existing store?

Yes. Products, customers, orders, reviews and URLs can be moved across, with redirects mapped so existing search rankings are not lost. We test the checkout thoroughly on staging before anything is switched over.

How is this different from dynamic web development?

E-commerce work centres on selling: catalogue, checkout, payments, shipping and stock. Dynamic development covers applications where the goal is not a transaction, such as portals, dashboards or booking systems. Some projects sensibly combine both.
