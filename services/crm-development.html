Common questions

Should we buy a CRM or have one built?

Buy, in most cases. An off-the-shelf CRM configured carefully covers what the majority of Australian businesses need, and somebody else maintains it. Custom CRM development is worth considering when your sales process genuinely differs from the standard shape, when integration with an unusual internal system matters more than features, or when licence costs across a large team have become the bigger expense.

Can you connect the CRM we already have?

Usually, yes. Most established platforms publish an API, which is a documented way for software to exchange data. CRM integration services typically connect accounting, website enquiry forms, email and job management, so a lead is entered once. Where a system has no API, we look at scheduled file transfers or a middle layer instead, and explain the limits before starting.

What happens to our existing customer data?

It gets migrated, and customer database development is usually the longest task in the project. We export what you hold, clean duplicates, map old fields to new ones and run a test load you can check before anything goes live. Customer records are personal information under the Privacy Act 1988, so we also agree who can see what, and how long inactive records are kept.
