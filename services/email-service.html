Common questions

Why do my emails go to customers' spam folders?

Usually because your domain lacks correct SPF, DKIM and DMARC records, or because sending practices trip filters. We audit your DNS and authentication, correct what is missing, and monitor results so legitimate mail reaches the inbox reliably.

Will I lose old emails when we move providers?

No. We migrate existing mailboxes over IMAP, including folder structures, before switching your MX records. Mail is verified in the new environment first, and the old service stays available until the migration is confirmed complete.

Can staff still use Outlook or their phones?

Yes. Mailboxes work with Outlook, Apple Mail, Android and iOS mail apps, and browser webmail. We provide the connection settings, help configure devices, and enable multi-factor authentication across every access method.
