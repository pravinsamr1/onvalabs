iOS App Development

What we deliver

Apple users expect a high standard. If your app is slow to launch, ignores the conventions of the platform or feels like a website in a wrapper, it gets deleted and rated accordingly. App Store review adds another hurdle: privacy manifests, App Tracking Transparency prompts, permission justifications and clear data-use disclosures all have to be right, and a rejection late in the project delays your launch.

We build iOS applications natively in Swift and SwiftUI, designed against Apple's Human Interface Guidelines so the app behaves the way your customers already expect. We also handle the submission process, so review requirements are addressed during the build rather than discovered at the end.

Common questions

Is iOS the right platform to start with?

iOS suits products aimed at higher-spending consumers, professional services clients and internal teams already issued iPhones or iPads. If your audience is broader or more price-sensitive, Android first or a cross-platform build usually delivers more reach per dollar.

How long does App Store review take?

Apple reviews most submissions within a few days, though timeframes vary and rejections happen. We reduce that risk by addressing privacy declarations, permission wording and guideline compliance before we submit, then respond quickly to any reviewer feedback.

Do we need separate iPhone and iPad versions?

Usually not separate apps, but the iPad experience does need deliberate design work to use the larger screen well. We scope that early so you can decide whether it earns its place in the first release.
