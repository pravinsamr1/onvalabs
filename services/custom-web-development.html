Common questions

How do we know custom is the right choice?

If a standard platform meets your needs with light configuration, use it. Custom makes sense when your process is genuinely unusual, when licence and workaround costs keep rising, or when the software itself is part of what you sell.

What will it cost and how long will it take?

Both depend on scope, so we quote after discovery rather than before. Projects are broken into stages with fixed deliverables, so you approve each phase and can pause between them instead of committing to everything at once.

Who owns the code?

You do. Source code, repositories and infrastructure are yours and documented at handover, built on open frameworks rather than a proprietary platform only we can maintain. That keeps your options open if you change providers later.
