Common questions

Can we message anyone on WhatsApp?

No. Australian law requires consent before you send commercial electronic messages, and Meta requires opt-in as well. We help you organise compliant consent capture, keep records of it, and honour opt-outs so your account stays in good standing.

What is the 24-hour customer service window?

When a customer messages you, you can reply freely with normal messages for 24 hours. Outside that window you must use a Meta-approved template. We build your template library so your team is never stuck waiting.

Is this different from social media marketing?

Yes. Social media builds awareness with audiences who do not know you yet. WhatsApp is a one-to-one channel for people who have already opted in, used for conversation, service and conversion.
