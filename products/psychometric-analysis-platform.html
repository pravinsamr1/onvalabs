Why it exists

Most assessment tools are a test engine with an admin screen bolted on

That is fine until you are a provider serving multiple client organisations, each on a different plan, each with their own entitlement, each subject to somebody’s privacy regime — and someone asks you to prove that a result was never seen by a person who should not have seen it.

✗What breaks at provider scale

  • Entitlement is a spreadsheet, so a client quietly consumes assessments they were never licensed for.
  • Every staff member can see every candidate result, because the permission model has two levels.
  • The audit log is a table that an administrator can edit.
  • Data residency is a promise in a contract rather than a property of the system.
  • Accessibility is an add-on SKU, and the item bank has no calibration story at all.

✓What Clarent does instead

  • Entitlement is enforced at the database. Allocating an unlicensed instrument raises insufficient_privilege at the trigger.
  • Row-level security on every tenant table, forced on the application role — clinical results require a clinical role.
  • The activity trail is append-only and hash-chained: every row carries the hash of its predecessor.
  • Each tenant is pinned to one of seven regions, and there is no cross-region processing.
  • WCAG 2.2 AA is native, and items move scaffold → authored → reviewed → calibrated before any real decision.

A command centre for the whole portfolio

Active tenants, instruments licensed, sessions in flight and units consumed across every client. Framework reach shows how far the catalogue actually penetrates your book.

Results that stay de-identified by design

An aggregate view where the role genuinely cannot resolve individual candidates. Percentile distribution against the norm set, standing bands, and filters that compose across framework, instrument, cohort, band, validity and time window. The interpretive note is built in: a spike at the ceiling usually means the form was too short, not that everyone did well.

Access with a boundary you can point at

Accounts exist for a role inside a defined scope and nowhere else — the boundary is the point of the screen. Client organisations, provider staff accounts and emergency break-glass accounts scoped to one named client site, with the functions chosen one by one and a second factor required on every account.

04

An activity trail that detects its own tampering

Append-only and hash-chained: every row carries the hash of its predecessor, so a deleted or edited event breaks the chain and is detectable. Chain status is verified and displayed. Seven-year retention. Filters compose across person, activity kind, exact action, instrument, allocation and organisation.

05

Security posture as a page anyone can read

All ten OWASP Top 10 categories answered at design level with abuse-case tests, plus the privacy and residency controls that matter for Australian education and health data. TLS 1.3 only, AES-256 at rest with per-tenant envelope keys in a managed HSM, signed artefacts with provenance attestation, and a deny-by-default egress posture.

An item bank with an honest calibration story

20,400 questions across 68 instruments and 469 scales, 300 per instrument, blueprinted by scale, format and difficulty tier. Formats span Likert, situational judgement, frequency, rater, forced-choice pairs and multiple choice. Scaffold items are labelled as scaffold — the workflow moves them to authored, reviewed and calibrated before an instrument is cleared for a real decision.

Questions

Straight answers

A platform. The candidate experience is one part of it; the rest is the provider console — catalogue, licensing, entitlement, tenancy, de-identified analytics, access governance and assurance. It is built for the organisation that publishes assessments, not only for the person sitting one.

Row-level security on every tenant table forced on the application role, candidates restricted to their own rows by a restrictive policy, clinical results gated behind a clinical role, and an aggregate results view where the role cannot resolve individuals. Response payloads and clinical results are additionally field-encrypted.

Honesty about calibration state. Scaffold items are structurally valid and runnable — scale-mapped, keyed, reverse-flagged, carrying seeded IRT parameters — but they are not calibrated against a norm sample. The workflow moves items scaffold, authored, reviewed, calibrated, and an instrument is not cleared for a real decision until it is through.

Yes. Each tenant is pinned to exactly one of seven regions, including AU-Southeast, and there is no cross-region processing. The region is a property of the tenant, set when it is created.

A break-glass account can be created against one named client site, with the functions chosen one by one. It is out-of-band by design, requires approval, and every action it takes is written to the hash-chained activity trail with alerting on the event.
