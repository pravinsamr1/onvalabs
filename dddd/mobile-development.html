Common questions

Should I build native or cross-platform?

It depends on your goals. Native gives the best performance and platform fit; cross-platform (Flutter / React Native) can cut time and cost when you need both platforms fast. We'll recommend the right path for your project.

How much does an app cost?

A focused MVP typically starts around $12,000. Feature-rich apps with backends, integrations and both platforms are quoted per scope after a discovery session.

Do you handle the app store submission?

Yes — we manage the full submission process for both the Apple App Store and Google Play, including review preparation and store listings.

Can you take over an existing app?

We can. We'll audit the codebase, stabilise it and pick up new feature development or maintenance from there.
