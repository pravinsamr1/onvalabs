Common questions

Should we have a page for every suburb we service?

Only where there is something distinct to say. A page about website development Blacktown projects should read differently from one about web development Norwest clients: the businesses, budgets and problems differ. Thin pages that repeat one another with the suburb swapped are ignored by search engines and readers alike. We usually start with a handful of genuine area pages and expand once they prove useful.

We are a trade business. What actually brings in calls?

SEO for tradies in Western Sydney comes down to unglamorous things done consistently: a page for each service written in the language customers use, an accurate Google Business Profile with photos of recent work, a site that loads quickly on mobile data, and a number people can tap. Reviews and clear service areas matter more than clever design here.

Do you work on site, or remotely?

Both. Early workshops are often easiest in person, and we travel across the region for them, including to offices around Castle Hill and the Norwest business park. After that most projects run remotely with scheduled reviews, which keeps costs down and progress visible. As an SEO company Western Sydney clients also use for ongoing work, we keep the same reporting rhythm after launch.
